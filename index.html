About The Owner

An emotional, trusted boutique story rooted in heritage, craftsmanship, and love.

Kasamma — A Family Boutique Of Timeless Elegance

Kasamma is more than a saree boutique — it is a cherished family legacy bringing together handcrafted weaves, Telugu cultural artistry, and the warmth of personal styling. Each drape is curated with devotion, honoring tradition, beauty, and the love every woman deserves to feel.

From bridal heirlooms to everyday grace, Kasamma promises trusted authenticity, premium craftsmanship, and heartfelt service.
